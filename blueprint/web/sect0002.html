#

Uses

  • Lemma 7.1

Lean declarations

  • PrimeSquares.RawGranvilleKurlbergInput

External input; no proof claimed. Assume the general CRT-family theorem taking local correlation and subpower hypotheses to joint cyclic Poisson gap limits. Its proof and exact cyclic joint bridge remain external. This quoted theorem is accounted for separately and is not used by Theorem 1.5(i).

Paper Theorem 8.6. quoted external theorem.

For each odd prime \(p\), let \(\Omega _p\subset \mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\), put \(r_p=|\Omega _p|/p\) and \(s_p=r_p^{-1}\), and define \(\Omega _q\) from the \(\Omega _p\), for squarefree \(q\), by the Chinese remainder theorem. Suppose that, for every fixed \(k\geq 1\) and all distinct \(0,h_1,\ldots ,h_{k-1}\pmod p\),

\begin{align} N_k(\mathbf h,\Omega _p) & :=\big|\{ t\in \mathbb {Z}/p\mathbb {Z}:t,t+h_1,\ldots ,t+h_{k-1}\in \Omega _p\} \big|=r_p^kp\big(1+O_k((1-r_p)p^{-c})\big) \end{align}

for some \(c{\gt}0\), and suppose that \(s_p=p^{o(1)}\). Then the normalized spacings of \(\Omega _q\) are Poisson distributed as \(s_q=q/|\Omega _q|\to \infty \). In particular, if the elements of \(\Omega _q\) are listed cyclically and \(F_q(\theta )\) is the proportion of normalized gaps exceeding \(\theta \), then \(F_q(\theta )\to e^{-\theta }\) for every \(\theta {\gt}0\).
