Paper Lemma 10.11. proved under source hypotheses.

Uniformly in \(\ell ,U,W\) and \(n{\lt}N^2\),

\[ H_{\ell ,R}(n;U,W)\geq 0, \qquad H_{\ell ,R}(n;U,W) = H_\ell (n;U,W)+O(NR^{-1/2}), \]

where

\[ H_\ell (n;U,W) = \int _{\substack {2\leq u\leq U\\ 2\leq (n-u^2)/\ell \leq W}} \frac{du}{\log u\log ((n-u^2)/\ell )}. \]
Proof architecture ▼

Construct the compactly supported smooth seed internally. Express the smoothing factor by autocorrelation, prove positivity of its mass, and compare the source singular integral with the positive model on the stated range. Quantify the discarded tail uniformly in the parameters needed by both sieve applications.

Paper Lemma 10.19. proved under source hypotheses.

The series

\[ \sum _{\substack {q\geq 1\\ P^+(q)\leq R}} \mathfrak G_q(\ell ,k,n) \]

converges absolutely and satisfies

\begin{align} \sum _{\substack {q\geq 1\\ \begin{bgroup} P^+(q)\leq R \end{bgroup}}} \mathfrak G_q(\ell ,k,n) & = \prod _{p\leq R} \left(1+\sum _{\nu \geq 1} \mathfrak G_{p^\nu }(\ell ,k,n)\right) =G_R(\ell ,k,n), \end{align}

where \(G_R\) is defined in (9.11). Moreover, for some absolute \(C_1{\gt}0\),

\begin{align} |\mathfrak s(a,q)| & \ll q^{-1/2}\tau (q)^{C_1},\notag \\ |\Upsilon (a\ell ,q,a_{k,\ell },k)| & \ll q^{-1}(q,k\ell )\tau (q)^{C_1},\\ |\mathfrak G_q(\ell ,k,n)| & \ll q^{-1}(q,k\ell )\tau (q)^{C_1},\notag \end{align}

and

\begin{align} \sum _{\ell {\lt}L_0}\sum _{k{\lt}V_\ell } \frac{(q,k\ell )}{\ell \phi (k)} \ll \tau (q)^{C_1}(\log N)^2. \end{align}
Proof architecture ▼

Group by reduced rational points and prove the prime-power evaluations, treating the dyadic factor separately. Establish absolute convergence before exchanging the smooth-denominator sum with the product. Bound the remaining gcd and divisor sums uniformly in the two coefficient indices; the local product is the one used by the actual representation problem.

Paper Lemma 9.10. complete conditional implication.

Let \(A,\delta {\gt}0\) and \(L_0{\lt}N^{2-\delta }\). There are sufficiently large constants \(B=B(A,\delta )\) and \(C=C(A,\delta )\) such that the following holds. Put

\[ R=(\log N)^C,\qquad \eta _N(\theta )=\eta \left(\frac{N^2\theta }{R}\right), \]

and suppose that \(V_\ell \leq (N^2/\ell )^{1/2}(\log N)^{-B}\) for \(\ell {\lt}L_0.\) Let \(|d_{k,\ell }|\leq 1\) and \((a_{k,\ell },k)=1\). Define

\begin{align} \quad \quad G_R(\ell ,k,n) & := \mathcal T_2(\ell ,k,n;a_{k,\ell }) \prod _{2{\lt}p\leq R} \frac{p\phi ((p,k))}{(p-1)^2} \left|\left\{ (u,v)\in \mathbb A_p^2: \begin{array}{l} u^2+\ell v\equiv n\pmod p,\\ v\equiv a_{k,\ell }\pmod{(p,k)} \end{array}\right\} \right|, \end{align}

where \( \mathcal T_2(\ell ,k,n;a_{k,\ell }) \) is defined in (3.18) and the second congruence is understood to be trivial when \(p\nmid k\). Also define

\begin{align} H_{\ell ,R}(n) := \ell \int _{\mathbb R}\eta _N(\theta )^2 \left(\int _2^N\frac{e(\theta u^2)}{\log u}\, du\right) \left(\int _{\substack {2\leq v\leq N^2\\ \begin{bgroup} \ell v{\lt}N^2 \end{bgroup}}} \frac{e(\ell \theta v)}{\log v}\, dv\right) e(-n\theta )\, d\theta . \end{align}

Then

\begin{align} \sum _{n{\lt}N^2}\left| \sum _{\ell {\lt}L_0}\sum _{k{\lt}V_\ell } d_{k,\ell }r_{k,\ell }(n)\right|^2 \ll _{A,\delta }N^4(\log N)^{-A}, \end{align}

where

\begin{align} r_{k,\ell }(n) & = \sum _{\substack {m^2+\ell t=n\\ \begin{bgroup} t\equiv a_{k,\ell }\pmod k \end{bgroup}}} \mathbf{1}_{\mathbb P}(m)\mathbf{1}_{\mathbb P}(t) - \frac{G_R(\ell ,k,n)}{\ell \phi (k)}H_{\ell ,R}(n). \end{align}
Proof architecture ▼

Combine the hybrid minor bound, actual major replacement, arithmetic completion and analytic smoothing. Choose the logarithmic exponents in an order compatible with the requested saving and the uniform range of the outer index. The final theorem concerns the full original representation sums, with the actual source remainder and singular integral.
