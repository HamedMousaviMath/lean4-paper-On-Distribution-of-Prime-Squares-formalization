Paper Lemma 11.6. proved under source hypotheses.

For \(p{\gt}3\), \(p\equiv 3\pmod4\),

\begin{align} \frac{\omega _n(p)}p = \begin{cases} \displaystyle \frac{\rho _p(n-1)}{p-1-\rho _p(n)},& p\leq R,\\[7pt] \displaystyle \frac1{p-1},& p{\gt}R. \end{cases}\end{align}

In particular, for \(p\geq 7\), \(0\leq \omega _n(p)/p\leq 2/(p-3){\lt}1\).

Paper Lemma 11.8. proved under source hypotheses.

There is a multiplicative function \(\omega _n(\ell ,\cdot )\), defined on square-free integers supported on \(W_\ell \), such that

\begin{align} \frac{G_R(\ell ,12d,n-1)}{\phi (12d)} = \frac{G_R(\ell ,12,n-1)}{\phi (12)} \frac{\omega _n(\ell ,d)}d . \end{align}

For \(p\nmid 12\ell \), its factors are

\begin{align} \frac{\omega _n(\ell ,p)}p = \begin{cases} \displaystyle \frac{\rho _p(n)}{p-1-\rho _p(n-1)},& p\leq R,\\[7pt] \displaystyle \frac1{p-1},& p{\gt}R. \end{cases}\end{align}

In particular, \(0\leq \omega _n(\ell ,p)/p{\lt}1\).

Proof architecture ▼

Compare the variable sieve sets with fixed supported prime sets, keeping the exceptional divisor contribution. Count the lower and upper residue classes explicitly and prove multiplicativity, nonzero base factors and all congruence restrictions. This supplies concrete supported densities to the general sieve interfaces rather than postulating their local formulas.

Paper Lemma 11.13. complete conditional implication.

Suppose that

\begin{align} |\mathcal F_d|=Xg(d)+r_d, \end{align}

where \(X\geq 0\), and \(g\) is multiplicative on the square-free integers supported on a fixed set of primes \(\mathfrak P\), with \(g(1)=1\) and \(0\leq g(p){\lt}1\). Fix \(C,A{\gt}0\), set \(R=(\log N)^C\), \(D_0=\exp ((\log \log N)^3)\), and \(D_1=D/D_0\), and assume \(R+1\leq z\leq D_1\leq N^A\) for sufficiently large \(N\). Suppose that fixed constants \(\kappa _0,\kappa {\gt}0\) and \(K_0\geq 2\) satisfy, for every \(2\leq w{\lt}y\leq z\),

\begin{align} \prod _{\substack {w\leq p{\lt}y\\ \begin{bgroup} p\in \mathfrak P,\ p\leq R \end{bgroup}}} (1-g(p))^{-1} & \leq \left(1+\frac{K_0}{\log w}\right) \left(\frac{\log y}{\log w}\right)^{\kappa _0},\notag \\ \prod _{\substack {w\leq p{\lt}y\\ \begin{bgroup} p\in \mathfrak P,\ p{\gt}R \end{bgroup}}} (1-g(p))^{-1} & \leq \left(1+\frac{K_0}{\log w}\right) \left(\frac{\log y}{\log w}\right)^\kappa . \end{align}

All \(o(1)\) terms below are uniform for fixed \(C,A,\kappa _0,\kappa ,K_0\). Then

\begin{align} S(\mathcal F,\mathfrak P,z) & \geq XV(z)\bigl(f_\kappa (s)+o(1)\bigr) +\sum _{m{\lt}D}c_m^-r_m,\notag \\ S(\mathcal F,\mathfrak P,z) & \leq XV(z)\bigl(F_\kappa (s)+o(1)\bigr) +\sum _{m{\lt}D}c_m^+r_m, \end{align}

where

\[ V(z)=\prod _{\substack {p{\lt}z\\ p\in \mathfrak P}}(1-g(p)), \qquad s=\frac{\log D_1}{\log z}, \qquad |c_m^\pm |\leq 1. \]

The coefficients \(c_m^\pm \) depend only on the fixed prime sets and the sieve levels, and not on the values of \(g\). The dimension \(\kappa _0\) is used only in the preliminary sieve over \(p\leq R\); the sieve functions in (11.15) are determined by the outer dimension \(\kappa \).

Proof architecture ▼

Choose the independent one-stage weights before the supported density. Combine lower and upper weights with the signed sandwich identity, using unique factorization across the disjoint prime ranges. Verify support below the full level and coefficient bound one. Prove the preliminary error tends to zero uniformly; the outer sieve functions retain dimension \(\kappa \). The revised source level condition implies the omitted strict inequality.

\begin{align} X_n\Theta _n(z)\gg _{A_0,C} N(\log N)^{-C_{A_0}} \end{align}

\begin{align} \sum _{\ell \in L}X_n(\ell )\Theta _{n,\ell }(z_\ell ) \leq \bigl(c_\alpha \mathcal W(\alpha )+o(1)\bigr) X_n\Theta _n(z), \qquad c_\alpha =\frac{16e^{-\gamma /2}}{\sqrt{\pi \alpha }}. \end{align}
