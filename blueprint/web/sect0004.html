Paper Theorem 4.1. proved under source hypotheses.

Let \(A_N\) and \(B_N\) be positive convolution operators whose kernels are supported in an interval of length \(O(N)\). Suppose that

\begin{align} |A_Nf(x)|& \leq (\log N)^aB_N|f|(x),\\ |B_Nf(x)|& \ll N^{-v}\| f\mathbf1_{[x-CN,x+CN]}\| _1, \qquad 0\leq v{\lt}1, \end{align}

and that \(s\geq 1-v\), \(r{\gt}0\), and, whenever \(2\leq J\leq N^{(1-v)/s}/10\),

\begin{align} B_N=Lo_{J,N}+Hi_{J,N},\qquad \| Lo_{J,N}f\| _\infty & \ll N^{-1}J^s(\log J)^\gamma \| f\| _1, \\ \| Hi_{J,N}f\| _2& \ll J^{-r}(\log J)^\beta \| f\| _2, \end{align}

where \(\gamma \geq \beta \geq 0\). Suppose also that, for \(2\leq Q\) and \(\log Q\leq c_0(\log N)^c\),

\begin{align} A_N& =\operatorname {Low}_{Q,N}+\operatorname {High}_{Q,N}+E_{Q,N}, \\ \| \operatorname {Low}_{Q,N}f\| _\infty & \ll N^{-1}Q^s(\log Q)^\alpha \| f\| _1,\\ \| \operatorname {High}_{Q,N}f\| _2 & \ll Q^{-r}\exp \left(c_1\frac{(\log Q)^\kappa }{\log \log Q}\right)\| f\| _2, \\ \| E_{Q,N}f\| _2& \ll _A Q^{-r}(\log Q)^{C_A} (1+\log N)^{-A}\| f\| _2 \qquad (A{\gt}0), \end{align}

where \(0{\lt}\kappa {\lt}1\) and \(a,\alpha ,c_0,c_1,c{\gt}0\). In (4.8), the constant \(C_A\) may depend on \(A\). Then, for \(F,G\) contained in an interval of length \(N\),

\begin{align} \frac{|\langle B_N\mathbf1_F,\mathbf1_G\rangle |}{N} & \ll (|F||G|/N^2)^{1-\frac{s}{2(s+r)}} (\operatorname {Log}(|F||G|/N^2))^{\gamma +\frac{s(\beta -\gamma )}{s+r}},\\ \frac{|\langle A_N\mathbf1_F,\mathbf1_G\rangle |}{N} & \ll (|F||G|/N^2)^{1-\frac{s}{2(s+r)}}\Phi _{c^*,\kappa }(|F||G|/N^2), \end{align}

where

\begin{align} c^*=\frac{c_1s}{2^\kappa (s+r)^{\kappa +1}}+o(1). \end{align}

Here and below the \(o(1)\) in a subscript means that the estimate holds with \(c^*\) replaced by any fixed larger constant.

Paper Corollary 4.15. proved under source hypotheses.

Assume in addition that \(A_N\) and \(B_N\) are uniformly bounded on \(\ell ^2\). If

\begin{align*} \frac{2(r+s)}{2r+s}{\lt}p\leq 2, \end{align*}

then, for \(f\in \ell ^p(\mathbb {Z})\),

\begin{align} \| B_Nf\| _{p'}+\| A_Nf\| _{p'} \ll _p N^{1/p'-1/p}\| f\| _p. \end{align}
Proof architecture ▼

Balance the low \(\ell ^1\! \to \! \ell ^\infty \) cost against the high \(\ell ^2\) cost. Separate admissible large scales from the complementary range controlled by the ordinary operator. Choose a strictly larger loss coefficient before choosing cutoffs and error exponents. Pass from indicators to strong improving by dyadic level decomposition and summable geometric estimates, including \(p=2\).

Paper Theorem 1.1. complete conditional implication.

Let \(I=[-N^2,2N^2]\cap \mathbb Z\). For all \(F,G\subseteq I\),

\begin{align} \frac1{|I|} \bigl|\langle A_{N,\Lambda }\mathbf1_F,\mathbf1_G\rangle \bigr| & \ll \left(\frac{|F|}{|I|}\frac{|G|}{|I|}\right)^{2/3} \Phi _{4\sqrt3/9+o(1)} \left(\frac{|F|}{|I|}\frac{|G|}{|I|}\right). \end{align}

Consequently, for every \(3/2{\lt}p\leq 2\) and every \(f\in \ell ^p(\mathbb Z)\) supported in \(I\),

\[ \left(\frac1{|I|}\sum _{x\in \mathbb Z} |A_{N,\Lambda }f(x)|^{p'}\right)^{1/p'} \lesssim _p \left(\frac1{|I|}\sum _{x\in \mathbb Z}|f(x)|^p\right)^{1/p}. \]
Proof architecture ▼

Apply the fixed-scale abstract theorem to the genuine von Mangoldt square average and the independent ordinary-square decomposition. The plus/minus square reflection is proved with unchanged constants. Keep the exact cardinality of \([-N^2,2N^2]\cap \mathbb Z\) in the normalization and handle small scales. This route does not require the smooth multifrequency maximal input.
