Paper Lemma 6.5. complete conditional implication.

If \((a,q)=1\), \(q\leq R^{11/10}\), and \(|\theta |\leq R/(qN^2)\), then

\begin{align} \widehat{\widetilde A}_{N,\Lambda }(a/q+\theta ) & =\mathfrak s(a,q)\widehat{\widetilde M}_N(\theta ) -\mathbf1_{q_0\mid q}\mathfrak s_{\chi _q}(a,q) \widehat{\widetilde M}_{N,\beta }(\theta ) +O(R^{-5}). \end{align}

Here \(q_0=q_0(N)\), \(\beta =\beta (N)\), and \(\chi _0=\chi _0(N)\) are the exceptional conductor, zero, and primitive character supplied by the Landau–Page theorem at scale \(N\), and \(\chi _q\) is induced by \(\chi _0\). These quantities may change when \(N\) changes. The second term is omitted if no exceptional character exists. If \(\xi \) lies outside all major arcs

\[ \left\| \xi -a/q\right\| \leq \frac{R}{qN^2}, \qquad q\leq R,\quad (a,q)=1, \]

where distance is taken modulo one, then

\begin{align} |\widehat{\widetilde A}_{N,\Lambda }(\xi )| \ll R^{-1/4}(\log N)^{C_0}. \end{align}

Here \(R\geq 1\), \(N\) is sufficiently large, and \(R^4\leq N/2\), as is the case for the choice in (6.4).

Paper Lemma 6.10. complete conditional implication.

For every \(A{\gt}0\),

\begin{align} \| E_{Q,N}f\| _2 \ll _A Q^{-1/2}(1+\log N)^{-A}\| f\| _2. \end{align}
Proof architecture ▼

Select compatible progression and quadratic exponential-sum data. On major arcs use the same exceptional datum as in the model; off the source arcs combine the rational approximation with the Ren and Ghosh bounds. Check periodized lifts and the bounded number of active translates. Absorb the intermediate power losses into arbitrary logarithmic decay. Both the major and minor cases are necessary.

Paper Proposition 6.16. complete conditional implication.

For nonnegative \(f\),

\begin{align} |\operatorname {Low}_{Q,N}f(x)| \ll Q\log (2Q) \sup _{M\geq N}M^{-2} \sum _{|y-x|\leq CM^2}f(y). \end{align}

Consequently,

\[ \| \operatorname {Low}_{Q,N}f\| _\infty \ll N^{-2}Q\log (2Q)\| f\| _1. \]
Proof architecture ▼

Invert the localized multiplier into the arithmetic factor times the smooth spatial kernel. Apply the corrected Euler product and its logarithmic bound, then sum spatial annuli to obtain the local density estimate. Taking the supremum of the same pointwise envelope gives the stated norm bound without an extra maximal loss.

Paper Lemma 6.18. complete conditional implication.

Let \(0{\lt}\beta \leq 1\), and let \(\mathcal R_s\) be any subset of the reduced fractions with denominators \(q\sim 2^s\). Then

\begin{align} \left\| \sup _{\substack {N\in 2^{\mathbb N}\\ \begin{bgroup} N\gtrsim 2^s \end{bgroup}}} \left| \left( \sum _{a/q\in \mathcal R_s} c_{a,q}\widehat{\widetilde M}_{N,\beta }(\xi -a/q) \eta _s(\xi -a/q)\widehat f(\xi ) \right)^\vee \right| \right\| _2 \ll (1+s)\sup _{a/q\in \mathcal R_s}|c_{a,q}|\, \| f\| _2. \end{align}

Paper Corollary 6.21. complete conditional implication.

Let \(0{\lt}\beta \leq 1\), \(s\geq 0\), and \(|c_{a,q}|\leq b\). Then

\begin{align} & \left\| \sup _{\substack {N\in 2^{\mathbb N}\\ \begin{bgroup} N\geq 2^s \end{bgroup}}} \sup _K \left| \left( \sum _{\substack {q\sim 2^s\\ \begin{bgroup} q\leq K \end{bgroup}}} \sum _{a\in \mathbb A_q} c_{a,q} \widehat{\widetilde M}_{N,\beta }(\xi -a/q) \eta _s(\xi -a/q)\widehat f(\xi ) \right)^\vee \right| \right\| _2 \ll (1+s)^2b\| f\| _2. \end{align}

The same estimate holds if each summand is multiplied by a fixed multiplier of modulus at most one supported where \(\eta _s(\xi -a/q)\neq 0\).

Proof architecture ▼

Transfer the independent smooth frequency estimate to the actual annular kernel. Separate the smooth main term from a square-summable remainder. For changing denominator cutoffs, use disjoint multiplier factorization and interval decompositions; retain the starting-scale restriction. The rational-period input and the general separated-frequency input have different logarithmic costs and are not interchangeable.

Paper Proposition 6.23. complete conditional implication.

Let \(N_0\geq 2\) and suppose that \(\log Q\leq c_0(\log N_0)^{1/2}.\) Then, for every dyadic \(N\geq N_0\),

\begin{align} \| \operatorname {High}_{Q,N}f\| _2 & \ll Q^{-1/2} \exp \left( (2+o(1))\frac{\sqrt{\log Q}}{\log \log Q} \right)\| f\| _2, \\ \left\| \sup _{\substack {M\geq N_0\\ \begin{bgroup} M\in 2^{\mathbb N} \end{bgroup}}} |\operatorname {High}_{Q,M}f| \right\| _2 & \ll Q^{-1/2} \exp \left( (2+o(1))\frac{\sqrt{\log Q}}{\log \log Q} \right)\| f\| _2. \end{align}
Proof architecture ▼

Group ordinary and exceptional coefficients by conductor and denominator scales. Page uniqueness gives at most one eligible character in each conductor class. Sum the resulting class estimates with the sharp arithmetic weights and pass from finite families to the countable supremum. The loss is uniform in the allowed starting scale, and bounded \(Q\) is absorbed separately.

Paper Proposition 4.19. complete conditional implication.

Let \(N\) be dyadic, let \(Q\geq 2\), and suppose that

\[ \log Q\leq c_0(\log N)^{1/2}, \]

where \(c_0{\gt}0\) is a sufficiently small absolute constant. Then

\begin{align} \widetilde A_{N,\Lambda } = \operatorname {Low}_{Q,N} +\operatorname {High}_{Q,N}+E_{Q,N}, \end{align}

where

\begin{align} \| \operatorname {Low}_{Q,N}f\| _\infty & \ll N^{-2}Q\log (2Q)\, \| f\| _1, \\ \| \operatorname {High}_{Q,N}f\| _2 & \ll Q^{-1/2} \exp \left( (2+o(1))\frac{(\log Q)^{1/2}}{\log \log Q} \right)\| f\| _2, \\ \| E_{Q,N}f\| _2 & \ll _A Q^{-1/2}(\log (2Q))^{O_A(1)} (1+\log N)^{-A}\| f\| _2 \end{align}

for every \(A{\gt}0\). More precisely, for some absolute \(C{\gt}0\), the low term satisfies

\begin{align} |\operatorname {Low}_{Q,N}f(x)| \ll Q\log (2Q)\sup _{M\geq N}M^{-2} \sum _{|y-x|\leq CM^2}|f(y)|. \end{align}

The maximal estimate needed in Section 5 is

\begin{align} \left\| \sup _{\substack {M\geq N_0\\ \begin{bgroup} M\in 2^{\mathbb N} \end{bgroup}}} |\operatorname {High}_{Q,M}f| \right\| _2 & \ll Q^{-1/2} \exp \left( (2+o(1))\frac{(\log Q)^{1/2}}{\log \log Q} \right)\| f\| _2, \end{align}

provided

\[ \log Q\leq c_0(\log N_0)^{1/2}. \]

Here \(o(1)\) is taken as \(Q\to \infty \), uniformly in the displayed ranges of \(N\) and \(N_0\); bounded values of \(Q\) are absorbed into the implicit constants. The operators in (4.20) are defined in Section 6.

Proof architecture ▼

Choose the Page-compatible raw data once and define all three operators from that selection. Assemble the pointwise low estimate, high norm and high maximum, and arbitrary-decay error for this same family. Prove exact operator identities in the relevant normed spaces; separately chosen existence witnesses would not establish the proposition.
