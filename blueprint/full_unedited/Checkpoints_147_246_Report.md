# Prime Squares: checkpoints 147–246

This batch contains **100 separately verified checkpoints**, adding **217 named supporting theorems in 100 modules**. The final project has **313 modules and 1321 named supporting theorems**. These are supporting-declaration counts, not counts of completed manuscript results.

The main advance is a genuine denominator-indexed circle low multiplier, with its inverse kernel identified and its local and all-input operator estimates proved in the stated natural-cutoff range. The batch also develops countable square-function and maximal estimates, including an applicable criterion for actual circle multipliers. **Theorem 1.1 remains open.**

## Mathematical results

1. **An actual smooth inverse cutoff.** The source smooth bump is proved to be Schwartz. Its inverse Fourier transform has rapid decay of every natural order. A proved real change of variables identifies the inverse transform of the frequency dilation with the physical dilation $a^{-1}\check\eta(x/a)$. Phase signs, complex scalars and normalizations are retained.

2. **The smoothed annular kernel.** For $N\ge2$, $\beta\le1$ and $0<a\le N^2$, the actual kernel

   $$K_{N,\beta,a}(x)=\frac1N\int_{N/2}^{N}t^{\beta-1}a^{-1}\check\eta\bigl((x-t^2)/a\bigr)\,dt$$

   is integrable in its defining variable and satisfies the proved rapid-decay envelope

   $$|K_{N,\beta,a}(x)|\le\frac{2^k C_k}{2a}\left(1+\frac{|x|}{N^2}\right)^{-k}.$$

   The constants $C_k$ come from the actual inverse cutoff. The argument includes the scale comparison between $x$ and $x-t^2$. It does not assume the desired spatial decay.

3. **Logarithmic arithmetic summation.** The ordinary truncated Cohen bound is assembled from the actual quadratic sums. For the exceptional arithmetic factor

   $$\rho(n)=\prod_{p\mid n}\frac{p+1}{p-1},$$

   prime-subset expansion, divisibility and reciprocal-multiple sums prove

   $$\sum_{n=1}^{D}\frac{\rho(n)}n\le e^2 H_D
   \le e^2\left(1+\frac1{\log2}\right)\log(2D),\qquad D\ge1.$$

   This estimate holds for all positive denominators; no squarefree restriction is needed for this upper bound. The correction Euler product is bounded by $e^2$. No PNT or Mertens hypothesis is used. The previously proved conductor-sensitive estimate for the actual induced twisted correlations supplies the exceptional coefficients.

4. **The ordinary minus exceptional physical low kernel.** The ordinary widths are $qN^2/Q$; the exceptional widths are $B_0dN^2/Q$, with $B_0=\texttt{conductorMajorant}(q_0)$. The arithmetic factor $B_0$ cancels against the exceptional smoothing width. For natural $N,Q\ge2$, a positive conductor, a quadratic base character, $\beta\le1$, $S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,Q\}$, and $B_0d\le Q$ for every $d\in S$, the actual complex kernel satisfies

   $$|L_{N,Q}(x)|\le C_{\mathrm{low}}\frac{Q\log(2Q)}{N^2}
   \left(1+\frac{|x|}{N^2}\right)^{-10}.$$

   The finite Gauss/unit sums and induced twisted coefficients are retained, with the exceptional term subtracted. The empty exceptional range is handled separately. The absolute constant is defined in Lean from the cutoff and arithmetic constants.

5. **Local convolution and genuine low operators.** A complete dyadic partition of integer distances includes every boundary and the inner shell. It gives, for every finite complex input, a convolution bound by $4C_{\mathrm{low}}Q\log(2Q)$ times the proved normalized dyadic local mass at scale $N^2$. The kernel also yields an all-input $\ell^1\to\ell^\infty$ bound

   $$\|L_{N,Q}\|_{\ell^1\to\ell^\infty}
   \le C_{\mathrm{low}}\frac{Q\log(2Q)}{N^2}.$$

   Finite-indicator pairings and far-field decay follow. Uniformly bounded families admit a genuine countable $\ell^1\to\ell^\infty$ maximum, including varying conductor, character and beta data between scales.

6. **The inverse-kernel identity for genuine circle symbols.** The compact frequency cutoff is passed to the circle, with the closed fundamental endpoints and every integer lift checked. Joint continuity and integrability justify the annular Fubini interchange. The resulting circle Fourier coefficient equals the actual smoothed physical kernel. Frequency shifts give the correct positive physical modulation. Summing over positive denominators and unit residues defines `denominatorCircleLow`, and its inverse kernel is proved equal to the physical low kernel above. The extra width budget $2Q\le N^2$ makes every contributing cutoff narrow enough for this circle construction. Thus the displayed local and $\ell^1$ estimates hold for this actual Fourier multiplier, not just an abstract kernel majorant.

   An all-input convolution series for every continuous circle symbol is also proved in $\ell^2$ through density/continuous linear maps, and its $\ell^1$ realization agrees with the actual $\ell^2$ multiplier on the inclusion of $\ell^1$. The `LowSymbolData` structure records only scale, character and width conditions; it contains no desired norm estimate. The exact all-real-lift formula for these circle symbols is recorded.

7. **Countable square functions and maxima.** For any sequence $F_j\in\ell^2(\mathbb Z)$ with $\sum_j\|F_j\|_2^2<\infty$, the double series of coordinate squares is summable. Its two orders of summation agree. The genuine pointwise square function is realized in real $\ell^2$ with

   $$\left\|\left(\sum_j|F_j|^2\right)^{1/2}\right\|_2^2
   =\sum_j\|F_j\|_2^2.$$

   It dominates the actual countable pointwise supremum, which is itself realized in $\ell^2$. Uniform finite energy bounds imply the countable maximal bound. This requires summability of squared output norms, a weaker condition than the earlier sum-of-output-norms construction. Operator versions, squared-operator-norm criteria, quantitative tail bounds, tail convergence to zero, sublinearity and Lipschitz control are proved.

8. **Actual annular collar decay and multiplier energy.** For $M\ge2$, $\beta\in[0,1]$, $q,Q>0$ and $|\theta|\ge Q/(2qM^2)$, the actual annular model at $2^jM$ satisfies

   $$|\mathcal A_{2^jM,\beta}(\theta)|
   \le\frac{4q}{\pi Q}4^{-j},\qquad
   \sum_{j\ge0}|\mathcal A_{2^jM,\beta}(\theta)|^2
   \le\frac{16}{15}\left(\frac{4q}{\pi Q}\right)^2.$$

   First collar onset and exact finite energy decomposition under disjoint frequency support are proved as well. For continuous circle symbols $m_j$, the explicit finite-frequency energy condition

   $$\sum_{j\in J}|m_j(\xi)|^2\le B^2
   \quad\text{for every finite }J\text{ and every circle point }\xi$$

   implies the genuine countable operator bound

   $$\left\|\sup_{j\ge0}|T_{m_j}f|\right\|_2\le B\|f\|_2.$$

   The implication is proved by integration, the Fourier isometry and the countable square-function construction. Pointwise attenuation of the symbols preserves this conclusion. **The frequency energy condition has not yet been verified for the paper's complete high/multifrequency family.** This criterion is supporting infrastructure, not a completed proof of those source estimates.

## Exact scope and remaining obligations

The new low estimates are unconditional within their displayed scale, character and finite-denominator hypotheses. The new square-function results are proved implications from their stated energy assumptions. No new deep analytic result, own-paper conclusion or mathematical axiom is introduced in this batch.

The low circle model uses natural $N,Q\ge2$ with $2Q\le N^2$ and the stated exceptional width budget. The general real-cutoff rounding and the exact identification with the earlier rational-indexed global approximation model remain open. The local result covers finite complex inputs; the global $\ell^1\to\ell^\infty$ result covers all $\ell^1$ inputs. These distinctions prevent a claim that every version and domain of the source low lemma 6.14 has been completed.

The earlier conditional global circle approximation and its countable dyadic maximal-error result remain valid under their explicit raw progression-prefix, Ren and Ghosh hypotheses. Those deep estimates have not been formalized here. The stronger printed minor formula in Lemma 6.5 and the complete exceptional-data/source summation correspondence remain open. The sharp high and multifrequency estimates 6.16/6.21, Proposition 4.19 and **Theorem 1.1 remain open**. The new low proof and generic square-energy criterion do not silently discharge these obligations.

The earlier ten completed numbered results remain recorded with their exact scopes: 3.1, 3.11, 3.12, accepted revised 3.14 and Corollary 3.16, original 3.20, 3.24, 3.27, abstract Theorem 4.1 and abstract Corollary 4.15. Lemma 3.21 retains its two earlier explicit PNT limits; no separate PNT formalization has been integrated. There is no claim of full-paper correctness or publication certification.

No new manuscript statement error has been established in this batch, so no mandatory LaTeX replacement is prescribed. The frozen manuscript, bibliography and two accepted source revisions remain unchanged. New deductions are explained in the Lean modules and individual proof notes.
