## What this continuation establishes

The earlier checkpoints complete the fixed-Q ordinary and exceptional collar estimates, including the signed original source collar, its conductor gain and changing-character conductor classes. They also prove the abstract time-and-prefix Rademacher–Menshov principle and exact source denominator-prefix identities. The extension 547–600 connects that prefix principle to the actual circle and integer Fourier multipliers:

- Circle multiplication and the integer multiplier action respect symbol products. Almost-everywhere circle representatives are handled explicitly.
- Pointwise disjoint continuous symbols give exactly orthogonal integer l2 outputs, even for different inputs. Finite sums and intervals have exact Pythagorean square-energy identities.
- Arbitrary complex weights preserve spectral disjointness; bounded weights have the displayed square-budget comparison.
- Finite lists are padded by exact zeros to a binary capacity. Padding changes neither their genuine prefixes nor their square budget. The depth is Nat.clog 2 L, proved at most Nat.log2 L+1, including L=0 and L=1.
- For a finite disjoint family m_i, one nonnegative real l2 function dominates every nonnegative real denominator cutoff. Its norm is at most (Nat.log2 L+2) times the square root of the exact output energy. If the symbols are bounded by A, the energy is at most A^2 times the input square norm, with no cardinality loss.
- An exact localizer identity m_i=m_i*p_i and disjoint localizers imply all off-diagonal products vanish and every interval symbol localizes to the sum of its own localizers. No unjustified assertion that a smooth localizer is an idempotent projection is made.
- Explicit interval majorants and localized norm budgets yield a true majorant over every time and every denominator prefix. A separate sufficient criterion constructs those interval witnesses from finite time square-energy bounds on the actual circle symbols. **Those frequency-energy bounds are hypotheses of the criterion, not established properties of the paper's full high family.**
- Rational symbols are grouped exactly by denominator. The original high and exceptional high source symbols retain their actual formulas in these identities.
- A band Q<den<=R is exactly the difference of prefixes at R and min(Q,R). Thus the band is correctly empty when Q>R, including conductor-dependent exceptional cutoffs. A single prefix witness controls every such difference with norm cost two.
- The interval-to-prefix bridge reaches actual capped source high operators with varying scales, characters, conductors and beta. A fixed cap M applies to the entire source set only when 0<=R<=M; this cap restriction is proved and retained. No maximum over all unbounded caps is inferred from it.

## A verified guard against an invalid maximal inference

The final four checkpoints prove a general obstruction using the existing actual Fourier convention. For j>=0, the continuous symbol exp(-2*pi*i*j*theta) defines an integer multiplier of operator norm at most one and sends the unit mass at zero exactly to the unit mass at j. Any real l2 majorant for the first N such outputs must have square norm at least N. Hence no real l2 function dominates the countable family on this one fixed input.

This is **a counterexample to the general inference from uniform individual operator bounds to a countable maximal bound**. It is not a counterexample to the manuscript theorem. It explains why the separate multifrequency/maximal argument is essential and cannot be replaced by the single-scale high estimate already proved.
