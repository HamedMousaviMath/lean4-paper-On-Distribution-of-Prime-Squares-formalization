## What is now proved

The actual complementary narrow cutoff is analyzed at fixed real $Q$. For dyadic scales $N_j=2^jM$ with natural $M\ge2$, the first nonzero collar scale is defined exactly. Every earlier term vanishes. After onset, the actual annular oscillatory factor has geometric decay, uniformly for arbitrary $\beta_j\in[0,1]$. Consequently its complete scalar square energy is summable and quantitatively bounded. This is a fixed-$Q$ argument; it does not require summable varying cutoffs.

The centered continuous symbol retains the actual broad source cutoff. Exact compact circle periodization accounts for every term of its three-term window. For a single center the finite circle square energy is at most $(24q/(\pi Q))^2$. Coefficients of norm at most one may change arbitrarily with scale. The countable maximum is constructed as a genuine nonnegative real $\ell^2$ function, with pointwise domination and a proved norm bound.

On each denominator block the broad source cutoffs have at most four active canonical rational centers, including the small denominators. The rational-block circle energy constant is $96bU/(\pi Q)$ for denominators at most $U$ and coefficients at most $b$. A weighted finite Cauchy–Schwarz argument sums denominator-block energies without an extra block-count factor. The complete collar family with coefficient envelope $Cq^{-\delta}$ has square energy bounded by $(K_\delta Q^{-\delta})^2$ and a genuine countable $\ell^2$ maximum.

The actual principal and twisted unit square Gauss sums discharge that arithmetic envelope for every $0\le\delta<1/2$. Ordinary and twisted characters, scale-dependent selectors and scale-dependent $\beta\in[0,1]$ are included. Exact identities identify the original ordinary high terms with this collar below their actual narrow cutoff. The original globally truncated source low family also has its proved countable $\ell^1\to\ell^\infty$ maximum throughout the displayed uniform source logarithmic range.

For a positive exceptional conductor $q_0$, put

$$B_0=\operatorname{conductorMajorant}(q_0),\qquad W=Qq_0/B_0.$$

The exceptional collar uses $W$, which need not equal $Q$. Its canonical frequencies are exactly $0\le r<1$, $\operatorname{den}(r)\le\lfloor W\rfloor$ and $q_0\mid\operatorname{den}(r)$. If $W<1$, the set is empty. For $\eta,\varepsilon\ge0$ with $\eta+\varepsilon<1/2$, actual Gauss decay and conductor divisibility give

$$q^{-(\eta+\varepsilon)}\le q_0^{-\varepsilon}q^{-\eta}.$$

The already proved uniform bound on $B_0/q_0$ then yields actual exceptional collar energy at most

$$(H_{\eta,\varepsilon}Q^{-\eta}q_0^{-\varepsilon})^2,$$

and the corresponding genuine countable maximum. This covers every $Q>0$. The source global-radius restriction is identified exactly with a bounded coefficient mask, so no artificial requirement $W\le R$ remains. Inactive exceptional data and conductors beyond the source radius give exactly zero exceptional collar.

For changing characters, the displayed numerical classification premise is

$$q_0(i)\ge2^i.$$

This is a **hypothesis on the family of conductors**, not a formalized Landau–Page zero theorem. With exactly one class selected at each dyadic time, finite times are partitioned by the selected class. The squared class constants sum geometrically. There is no assumption that the selected class is monotone or stays active on a common tail.

Combining the ordinary and exceptional contributions proves a genuine maximum for the **signed original source collar** for every $0\le\eta<1/2$:

$$\left\|\sup_j |C_{N_j,Q}f|\right\|_2\le K_\eta Q^{-\eta}\|f\|_2.$$

Here $Q\ge1$, $M\ge2$, the source radii satisfy $Q\le R_j$, the active flags and character data can vary, $\beta\in[0,1]$, and the conductor-class condition above is retained. The Lean witness equals the pointwise countable supremum and dominates every individual output. Finite-indicator pairings have the corresponding $K_\eta Q^{-\eta}\sqrt{|F|}\sqrt{|G|}$ bound.

The continuous source model uses the already formalized broad-times-narrow low cutoff. Its identification with the printed cutoff-difference decomposition retains the earlier source geometry and exact low-symbol bridge. The extended symbol estimates at smaller nonnegative radii, where allowed by their Lean signatures, do not assert that printed-source identification.

**This controls the collar contribution only.** It does not establish the sharp full high bound $Q^{-1/2}\exp((2+o(1))\sqrt{\log Q}/\log\log Q)$, the remaining large-denominator maximum, or the headline theorem.

## Prefix theorem and proposed proof repair

A checked two-denominator example shows that a source-radius cutoff in one denominator block need not be one common time tail: denominators $2$ and $3$ at radii $2$ and $3$ have activation rows $(1,0)$ and $(1,1)$. Each individual denominator does have a tail under increasing radii. The full block is a denominator prefix. Exact finite identities prove the natural/real cutoff floors and the rational grouping by denominator, including the genuine circle major-arc model and exceptional denominators $q_0d$.

A finite binary tree argument now proves the Rademacher–Menshov prefix step. Every scalar prefix square is bounded by $(h+1)$ times the actual binary-tree energy. The associated square-root envelopes are constructed in real $\ell^2$, with exact spatial-total and norm identities. Uniform interval norm budgets give a logarithmic $(h+1)$ prefix norm cost. The orthogonal $\ell^2$ specialization is fully discharged. A second version dominates **every time and every denominator prefix simultaneously**, from explicitly displayed interval maximal majorants and their norm budgets.

The paper's underlying multifrequency interval estimate, its transference and its full source application are still open. They are not introduced as axioms or silently replaced by the prefix conclusion.

The proposed repair to the proof of Proposition 6.21 replaces the common-tail step by the manuscript's existing Corollary 6.19 (`c:orlicz-prefix`). The ordinary factor becomes $(1+s)^2$, and the exceptional large-denominator factor becomes $(1+\log(q_\chi d))^2$. These add only fixed logarithmic powers. The exact sharp-exponent absorption is proved in Lean: for every $\epsilon>0$ and fixed natural $n$, uniformly for $Q>0$ and $\log Q\ge2$,

$$Q^{-1/2}(1+\log Q)^n\exp\!\left(2\frac{\sqrt{\log Q}}{\log\log Q}\right)
\le D_{n,\epsilon}Q^{-1/2}\exp\!\left((2+\epsilon)\frac{\sqrt{\log Q}}{\log\log Q}\right).$$

The replacement changes a proof justification, not the stated conclusion. It is saved separately as `proposed_revisions/Replacement_6_21_Denominator_Prefix.tex`; the frozen manuscript and both previously accepted revisions are unchanged. It remains a proposed local repair, not a certificate of the entire proposition.
