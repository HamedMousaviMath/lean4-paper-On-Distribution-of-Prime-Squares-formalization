# Checkpoint 381: Assembly of distinct finite square energies with their correct total constant

If each finite block has time energy at most B_i^2, the full sum has energy at most (sum B_i)^2. The proof interchanges the two finite sums and cancels the positive weights; no factor equal to the number of blocks is introduced.
