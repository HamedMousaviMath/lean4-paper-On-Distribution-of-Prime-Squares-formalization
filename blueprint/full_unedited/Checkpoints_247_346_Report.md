## The actual source low symbol

The canonical source rational set is identified exactly with the positive-denominator/unit-residue double sum. The map $u\mapsto u/q$ has its reduced numerator and denominator proved, including the denominator-one center zero. A concrete finite equivalence gives exact reindexing of complex sums and the totient cardinality identity. Induced characters, optional exceptional selectors, Gauss coordinates and real frequency shifts agree with the original source definitions.

For a real cutoff $Q$, integer floors enumerate the denominators only. The real $Q$ itself stays in the smoothing widths. The ordinary width is $qN^2/Q$. For the exceptional conductor $q_0$, let $B_0=\texttt{conductorMajorant}(q_0)$; the exceptional width is $B_0dN^2/Q$. The contributing exceptional set is exactly

$$\{d\in\mathbb N:1\le d\le\lfloor Q\rfloor,\ B_0d\le Q,\ q_0d\le M\}.$$

All cutoff equality cases, natural/real conductor quotients, the exceptional minus sign and the inactive case are included. The actual induced correlation bound cancels $B_0$ against the exceptional width. The weighted arithmetic sum is bounded logarithmically using the previous elementary result; no PNT or Mertens assumption enters this low argument.

The inverse kernel is identified for the actual continuous circle symbol, first term by term and then for the complete sum. Exact all-real integer periodization and frequency-shift identities retain the normalizations and modulation signs. The combined ordinary-minus-exceptional model is proved equal to the original rational-indexed source low model. No desired kernel bound is assumed to obtain this identity.

The working radius may be enlarged to $R^2$ while the actual source denominator cap remains $\lfloor R\rfloor$. If $q_0>R$, every exceptional selector on the source set is exactly zero and the exceptional denominator set is empty. These facts remove the intermediate exceptional-width range restriction and cover **every positive conductor**.

For natural $N\ge2$, real $Q\ge2$, $Q\le R$, $2R^8\le N^2$, a positive conductor, a quadratic base character, an optional exceptional term and $\beta\le1$, the actual source low kernel satisfies

$$|L_{N,Q}(x)|\le 2C_{\rm low}\frac{Q\log(2Q)}{N^2}
\left(1+\frac{|x|}{N^2}\right)^{-10}.$$

Its genuine all-input operator and finite-complex local estimates are

$$\|L_{N,Q}\|_{\ell^1\to\ell^\infty}
\le 2C_{\rm low}\frac{Q\log(2Q)}{N^2},$$

$$|L_{N,Q}f(x)|\le 8C_{\rm low}Q\log(2Q)\,
\texttt{dyadicLocalMass}(N^2,F,|f|,x),\qquad \operatorname{supp}f\subseteq F\text{ finite}.$$

Here $\texttt{dyadicLocalMass}$ is the exact normalized integer-shell quantity already defined in Lean. The absolute constant is constructed from the actual cutoff and arithmetic constants. Finite-indicator pairings and a genuine countable $\ell^1\to\ell^\infty$ maximum follow, including families with varying exceptional data. The data structures record numerical and character conditions; they contain no desired norm conclusion.

The source paper budget $R^4\le N/2$ implies the displayed geometry. For the actual source choice

$$R_{N,Q}=\max\{\exp((\log N)^{1/10}),Q^8\},$$

the low estimates therefore hold uniformly for all sufficiently large $N$ and real $Q\ge2$ satisfying

$$\log Q\le \frac{\min\{c/7,1\}}8\sqrt{\log N},\qquad c>0.$$

These low proofs use no new deep analytic input. The local conclusion is for finite complex inputs; the global norm conclusion is for all $\ell^1$ inputs. Natural $N\ge2$ and real $Q\ge2$ are retained. We do not claim every domain or formulation of the printed low proposition (`p:lowcontribution`) beyond those proved hypotheses.

## The actual single-scale high bound

For **every fixed $0<\eta<1/2$**, there is $H_\eta>0$ such that the actual source high circle symbol and its genuine $\ell^2$ multiplier satisfy

$$\sup_\theta|m^{\rm high}_{N,Q}(\theta)|\le H_\eta Q^{-\eta},
\qquad \|H_{N,Q}\|_{\ell^2\to\ell^2}\le H_\eta Q^{-\eta}.$$

This holds for natural $N\ge2$, real $Q>0$, $\beta\in[0,1]$, every positive conductor, every finite canonical rational set with bounded denominators, arbitrary Dirichlet-character data and source coefficients of norm at most one. The constant is independent of the denominator cap, conductor, character family and arithmetic length. The actual optional source selector satisfies the coefficient condition by a proved theorem. The uniform estimate is for the defined continuous complement `circleHighModel`, whose narrow low term retains the broad cutoff factor. Its identification with the printed low/high decomposition uses the proved source low identity and source geometry above; that identification is not asserted outside those hypotheses.

The proof is elementary relative to the already proved Gauss bounds:

1. For every $\delta<1/2$, the principal and twisted unit Gauss sums have a uniform $C_\delta q^{-\delta}$ bound. A proved estimate also gives a universal upper bound on $B_0/q_0$.
2. The actual high mask is written exactly as $1-\mathbf1_{q\le W}\eta(qN^2\theta/W)$, with ordinary $W=Q$ and exceptional $W=Qq_0/B_0$. Its nonzero onset and the actual annular oscillatory estimate give both size and $q/W$ bounds.
3. Interpolation gives a centered high bound $D Q^{-\eta}q^{-(\delta-\eta)}$. The broad source cutoff is retained on both sides; no unjustified containment of broad and narrow cutoffs is used.
4. For each denominator block $s=\log_2q$, the broad cutoffs have at most four active canonical centers. For $s\ge2$ there is at most one; the small denominators are counted separately. Every inactive contribution is proved zero.
5. The retained factor satisfies $q^{-(\delta-\eta)}\le(2^{-(\delta-\eta)})^s$. Its geometric sum is bounded by $(1-2^{-(\delta-\eta)})^{-1}$ independently of the global denominator cap.
6. Exact three-term periodization on $[-1,1]$ passes the real sum to the circle. The resulting explicit constant is $12D/(1-2^{-(\delta-\eta)})$. Choosing $\eta<\delta<1/2$ and discharging the Gauss and conductor budgets proves the claim.

Finite-indicator high pairings satisfy $H_\eta Q^{-\eta}\sqrt{|F|}\sqrt{|G|}$. For a sequence of actual high operators with varying cutoffs $Q_j$ and arbitrary other allowed data, the proved condition $\sum_j Q_j^{-\eta}<\infty$ yields a genuine countable pointwise supremum in $\ell^2$, with norm at most $H_\eta(\sum_j Q_j^{-\eta})\|f\|_2$. Geometric cutoffs $Q_j=2^jQ$ satisfy this summability by a separate proved calculation.

**These results do not supply the sharp printed high estimate or the fixed-cutoff countable high/multifrequency estimate.** The printed factor is $Q^{-1/2}\exp((2+o(1))\sqrt{\log Q}/\log\log Q)$, which is stronger than the proved $Q^{-\eta}$ bounds. The varying-cutoff maximum has the explicit summability condition; it must not be substituted for the paper's remaining high maximal statement.

## Conditional actual prime-square assembly

The remainder is defined as the actual circle high operator plus the actual global prime major-arc error operator. The annular von Mangoldt square kernel has an exact finite-input decomposition into the actual low output and this remainder output. The low input lies in $\ell^1$ and the remainder input in $\ell^2$; their finite coordinate agreement is checked.

The earlier conditional approximation bound gives an error norm at most

$$E_A(N,Q)=2\cdot2^A Q^{-1/2}(1+\log N)^{-A},\qquad A\ge0.$$

The new high bound and a proved absorption $E_A(N,Q)\le 2\cdot2^A Q^{-\eta}$ give an actual remainder norm $H Q^{-\eta}$ throughout the uniform source range. The **raw progression-prefix, Ren and Ghosh estimates remain explicit hypotheses** in `CircleAnalyticInputs`. That structure contains beta and coefficient conditions and those raw estimates, not the source high norm or the desired restricted pairing conclusion.

For the actual annular von Mangoldt square kernel $K_N$, every finite $F,G\subset\mathbb Z$ therefore satisfies the conditional result

$$|\langle K_N*\mathbf1_F,\mathbf1_G\rangle|
\le 2C_{\rm low}\frac{Q\log(2Q)}{N^2}|F||G|
+H Q^{-\eta}\sqrt{|F|}\sqrt{|G|}.$$

The quantifiers retain $0<\eta<1/2$, fixed nonnegative analytic/error parameters, a positive decay parameter $c$, sufficiently large natural $N$, the displayed real-$Q\ge2$ logarithmic range, every positive conductor and optional quadratic exceptional data satisfying the raw analytic inputs. The bound is derived from the exact operators and their proved estimates; no scalar pairing estimate is assumed.
