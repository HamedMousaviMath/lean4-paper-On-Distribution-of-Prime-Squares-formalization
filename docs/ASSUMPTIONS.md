The public results are conditional implications. Explicit hypotheses do not appear as extra constants in `#print axioms`; their truth and precise formulation bridges still need mathematical review. The groups in the blueprint compress this catalogue, not the assumptions themselves.

The 22 proved / 31 conditional / 1 quoted-external ledger counts numbered paper results, not independent input predicates. Some groups contain more than one predicate. Retired and internally proved entries are marked below.
